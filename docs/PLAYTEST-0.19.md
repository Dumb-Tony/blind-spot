# New forces — 0.19

Version 0.19 preserves the first 160 level identities and appends forty installations across Magnet Mile and Updraft. The campaign now contains 200 levels, ten regions, eight rebels and eight tools.

June's Cable Cutter replaces the Pull Hook. It removes one nearby cable or hinge, slices glass, heavily damages wood, chips concrete, and cuts unbolted camera mounts within 92 pixels. Fixed steel remains immovable and sends the cutter onward. The internal `grapple` identifier remains for compatibility with existing saves and recorded routes.

Vale's Magnet Puck attracts dynamic blocks and unbolted cameras inside 175 pixels. Tess's Airburst Capsule applies the opposite force inside 165 pixels. Both use distance falloff, wake sleeping structures and leave fixed steel unchanged.

Validation includes the exact production simulation, every recorded campaign route, initial-layout stability checks for all 200 levels, material fracture tests, floating-object regressions, inventory tests, tool-specific force assertions, 600 physics stress runs and the browser replay harness.
